# README evidence addendum

The objective, physical flute results, and downstream sparse-consumer claims are supported. Apply the scope clarifications below before publication.

- **Compute comparison:** the direct negative result is the earlier TSH1 resident-compute campaign, not the current TSH2 eight-second profile. Six 1,024-sample jobs had laptop medians 0.28330-0.41865 ms versus FPGA 1.77048 ms (88,524 cycles at 50 MHz), or 4.23-6.25 times the laptop median. CPU and FPGA campaigns were separate; these are ratios of medians, not paired runs. The 396 raw rows were independently recomputed. Source hashes and all six cases are in `review_addendum.json`. The previous 18-claim matrix did not itself bind this direct proof.
- **Application partition:** the four natural recordings were held out during software threshold selection. Subsequent hardware runs replicate that fixed set; two repeats do not create unseen recordings or independent application samples.
- **Timing wording:** strict packet validation and checksum handling are inside the decoded receiver timing. Numerical comparison against expected features or monitor output is after each timed batch. Loading/context creation, network, disk and upstream FPGA execution are outside. The monitor v02 timing uses software-reference spectra; its campaign did not retain a CPU model, so do not assign the neighboring i7-8750H metadata to it.

Natural quality gates apply separately to every recording: at least 99% of nonzero baseline groups must retain a valid dominant bin within one bin (187.5 Hz); centroid absolute error p95 must be at most 187.5 Hz, with no invalid thresholded centroid against a valid baseline; active-band relative-energy error p95 must be at most 10%; and the worst individual band's p95 must be at most 20%. A baseline-active band has group energy at least `max(1e9, 1e-4 * group total energy)`. Additional zero-input activity must be absent. At the selected threshold `1e7`, all four recordings retained 100% within-one-bin peaks; maxima across recordings were 1.7164 Hz centroid error p95, 1.1128% active-band error p95, and 2.8990% worst-band error p95. Natural events matched three threshold-zero clusters with zero additions or misses; this is agreement, not labeled detection accuracy.

Synthetic gates require all five intended persistent events across eight controls to contain the correct band member by onset plus 0.512 s, with no extra clusters, including silence, steady tones and a brief transient. These checks also apply at threshold zero. Natural cluster comparison uses baseline primary-band membership and an unused nearest onset within 0.256 s; it reports misses, additions and delay.

Each physical transaction contains 384,000 inputs and 3,001 spectral frames. Natural source support is 240,000 samples (five seconds), with a 1,876-frame byte prefix and a 1,785,952-byte dense comparator. Synthetic source support is 245,760 samples (5.12 seconds), with 1,921 frames and a 1,828,792-byte dense comparator. The physical eight-second dense comparator is 2,856,952 bytes. The appended 144,000 natural or 138,240 synthetic zeros are an interface accommodation. Compare original-support bytes and quality on the original duration, never an eight-second padded reduction presented as a five-second result.

Quality uses interior frames grouped into complete 48-frame windows, giving 39 natural groups. The physical prefix is checked byte for byte against the original finite-reference packets and against its power, feature and monitor outputs. Natural PCM16 and packed12 comparisons use 480,000 and 360,000 bytes, respectively. The CPU-only task-summary alternative is 6,552 bytes, is not emitted by the FPGA, and its encoding is not timed. TSP1 bytes exclude the separate reconstructed waveform and network framing.

Suggested sentence: **In an earlier matched resident-compute comparison of six 1,024-sample jobs, the TSH1 FPGA implementation took 4.23-6.25 times the i7-8750H laptop median time. This is separate from the current TSH2 eight-second capture and sparse-consumer results.**
